Questions, answered

Is my health data private?

Yes. Your entries stay strictly on your device or in your personal private encrypted backup. Zero third-party trackers, zero advertising, zero data brokering.

What can I track in Lunera?

Hormone levels and pharmacokinetic simulations, injection sites with tissue rest rotation, schedules & dosage alarms, physical body measurements, supply inventory, and mood journal entries.

Is Lunera free to download?

Yes. Lunera is 100% free and open-source under the MIT License. You can download the standalone APK directly from GitHub Releases and begin right away.

Does it work offline without an internet connection?

Absolutely. Lunera is built offline-first. All alarms, schedules, pharmacokinetic level simulations, body measurements, and local JSON backups function without an internet connection.
