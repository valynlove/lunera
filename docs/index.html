All your therapy rhythm, in one place

Built around the real needs of living with hormone therapy every day.

Flexible schedules

Daily, weekly, monthly, every N days, or as needed. Nine ways to take your medication, from injections to patches, with rolling cycle intervals counting from your last dose.

Reminders right on time

High-priority notifications at the exact scheduled time, even completely offline. Plus smart alerts when medication supplies run low or are about to expire.

Estimated levels

Multi-compartment pharmacokinetic curves estimated from your injection or oral entries, layered with actual blood lab results and target ranges set with your doctor.

Supplies that manage themselves

Vials, syringes, needles, and alcohol wipes deduct automatically every time you log a dose. Logged by mistake? Delete the entry and the stock returns automatically.

Body & clothing sizing

Track full bust, underbust, waist, hips, thighs, and feet. Includes scientific dual-standard bra cup sizing (US/UK & EU/ID), sister sizes, clothing fit, and shoe conversion.

Native Android widgets

Glanceable home screen widgets for your HRT therapy duration, next injection countdown, and hormone curves, dynamically translated to your active language.

Privacy in your hands

Lock the app with salted SHA-256 PIN or biometric fingerprint / face unlock. Your data is accessible solely by your account with zero ads and zero third-party trackers.

Dual cloud & offline backup

Seamless real-time synchronization via Firebase Cloud Firestore alongside full offline JSON file export/import. Switching phones never means starting from zero.

A calming look

Soft nocturnal sanctuary with gentle pastel highlights and numbers you can read at a single glance.
