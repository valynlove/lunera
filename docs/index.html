✦ Gentle Guidance ✦

Questions,
answered

Everything you need to know about privacy, offline reliability, and how Lunera keeps your personal health journey safe.

Is my personal health data completely private?

Yes. Your health data stays strictly on your device or in your personal private backup. There is zero telemetry, zero advertising SDKs, and zero third-party data broker integration. You can use Lunera with 100% features without creating an external cloud account.

What clinical parameters can I track in Lunera?

Lunera tracks hormone levels and multi-compartment pharmacokinetic simulations, injection sites with tissue rest rotation, oral/sublingual/patch/gel administration, laboratory blood markers, physical body measurements, supply inventory, and mood & symptom journals.

Is Lunera completely free to download and use?

Yes. Lunera is 100% free and open-source under the MIT License. There are no paywalls, subscriptions, or hidden charges. You can download the standalone APK directly from GitHub Releases and begin tracking immediately.

Does the app function offline without internet access?

Absolutely. Lunera is built offline-first. All alarms, schedules, pharmacokinetic level simulations, body measurements, and local JSON backups function without an internet connection. Cloud synchronization via Cloud Firestore is completely optional.

How does Lunera calculate estimated hormone levels?

Lunera utilizes peer-reviewed pharmacokinetic models from scientific literature for common estradiol and testosterone formulations. The simulation estimates steady-state concentrations based on dose, interval, and administration route, calibrated against your verified blood test laboratory results.
