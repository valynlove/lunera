Core Features

Engineered for Velocity, Elegance & Privacy

All the essential tools you need to accompany and understand your physiological journey without compromises.

⚡

Offline-First & Instantaneous

Built with an offline-first architecture. Launch Lunera and log doses, journals, and inventories instantly with zero network delay or buffering.

🔒

PIN Security & Multi-Device Sync

Protect private medical data with salted SHA-256 PIN credentials. Sync across all your devices via Cloud Firestore without setting up PINs again.

📈

Pharmacokinetic Level Curves

Interactive multi-compartment pharmacokinetic models for Estradiol (EV, EC, EB, EEn) and Testosterone, with customizable target ranges and blood test calibration.

📅

Dynamic Schedules & Timeline

Visual calendar picker navigation, rolling interval scheduling, and automatic dose reminders with exact historical intake logs.

🔔

Exact Alarm Reminders

Reliable offline Android local notifications with custom channels, high importance, and precise scheduling so you never miss an injection.

📖

Transition & Mood Journal

Daily emotional state scoring, physical transition observation notes, and categorized wellness entries designed for mindful reflection.

📦

Medical Supply Inventory

Track vials, syringes, alcohol wipes, and needles with automatic deduction upon dose intake and proactive low-stock warnings.

🌙

Midnight Celestial Aesthetics

A soothing dark theme with animated lake landscapes, orbit ring iconography, and dynamic accent palettes (Lunera, Moonlight, Ocean, Forest).

🌐

Bilingual Localization

Full native support for English (Default) and Bahasa Indonesia with seamless runtime switching across all interfaces and date formatters.
