All your therapy rhythm, in one place

Designed around the clinical reality and personal dignity of living with hormone therapy every single day.

Flexible Schedules

Daily, weekly, monthly, every N days, or as needed. Nine distinct routes of administration with rolling cycle intervals counting from your last logged dose.

Site Map & Tissue Rest

Interactive 2D anatomical silhouette with dual Front and Back perspectives. Tracks tissue recovery days per site and automatically suggests rotation to prevent tissue fatigue.

Guided Calm Injections

A step-by-step guided procedure designed for serenity and safety. Walks through sanitization, needle prep, steady injection pacing, and proper sharps disposal.

Precision Offline Reminders

Exact scheduled alarms delivered reliably via high-priority notification channels without needing an internet connection. Low-supply and expiry warnings keep you prepared.

Pharmacokinetic Simulations

Multi-compartment pharmacokinetic release curves estimated from your injections, sublingual, or transdermal doses, paired with blood lab history and clinical target ranges.

Auto-Managed Inventory

Vials, syringes, drawing needles, and alcohol wipes deduct automatically upon each dose. Accidentally logged? Removing the log cleanly restores inventory balances.

Body Metrics & Bra Sizing

Track full bust, underbust, waist, hips, and silhouette milestones. Includes dual-standard bra sizing (US/UK & EU/ID), sister-size charts, and shoe conversion.

Native Glanceable Widgets

Home screen Android widgets displaying therapy duration, injection countdown dials, and dynamic hormone level curves translated to your active language.

Zero-Knowledge Biometric Lock

Lock your health logs behind a PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 PIN or native biometric fingerprint/face authentication. Zero third-party trackers, zero data brokering, zero ads.

Dual Cloud & Offline Backup

Seamless real-time synchronization via encrypted Firebase Cloud Firestore alongside full offline JSON export and import. Your medical data stays strictly in your control.

An elevated nocturnal sanctuary

Gold-framed glassmorphism, celestial astrology iconography, and high-contrast clinical metrics you can digest at a glance.
